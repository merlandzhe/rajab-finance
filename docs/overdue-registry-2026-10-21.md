# HESAP — manual overdue registry

Implemented in the existing project. Merchant owners can add a report from a customer card only when a signed/transferred contract has overdue unpaid schedule amounts. Pending payment claims block publication until reviewed. Reports are not created automatically. The operation records the authenticated actor, contract/customer linkage, phone snapshot, amount, cutoff date and the owner's reference to the basis for sharing. The checkbox records the owner's attestation; it does not independently validate a legal basis.

Connected, active merchant owners can search by an exact normalized Russian phone number (30/day/user, enforced in SQL). No bulk directory or export is provided. Search exposes the reporting shop, masked name, reported/current arrears and dispute status, never other companies' contract IDs or evidence. Telephone matching is not identity verification. There is no automatic credit decision.

Customer profile includes own reports and a dispute action. Disputes notify the reporting company's owners and platform administrators through the existing durable notification queue. The reporting owner or platform administrator can withdraw a report with a reason. Buyers cannot withdraw a report, merchants cannot withdraw another company's report. Withdrawal excludes it from shared search while preserving internal history. Telegram delivery requires an eligible linked account that has started the bot.

Current arrears are recalculated from confirmed payment allocations for installments already overdue when the report was filed. Payment claims do not reduce debt. Once those arrears are paid the report displays resolved; later installments are not automatically added to this report. A reversal of a previously confirmed payment is reflected in the balance. Reported amount remains as history. Records in this first version are capped at the most recent 100 per view.
